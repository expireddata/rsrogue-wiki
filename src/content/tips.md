# Getting started

## Your first minutes

- **Use your starter key right away.** Open any chest and choose Melee, Ranged or Magic. Your choice
  sets which gear and lamp skills come up more often for the rest of the run.
- **Eat purple sweets** when hurt. They heal 1-3 each, and you have 25.
- **Pick up keys.** Bloody keys (12% per kill) and bloodier keys (3%) open chests for
  [modifiers](#/modifiers), gear and xp.
- **Search supply crates.** 5 per world, no key needed, and they speed up the early game.
- **Bury bones** for Prayer xp. Prayer is what makes TzTok-Jad beatable.
- **Explore.** Chests are bunched in pockets, and exploring the map is rewarded.

## Commands

Available to every player:

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `::reset` | Moves every member of the run to a new run |
| `::join <name>` | Joins another player's run. Only runs still in world 1 can be joined |
| `::invincible` / `::vincible` | Toggles taking 0 damage. For testing |
| `::world5` | Skips straight to the final world. For testing |

## Prayer and potions

Protection prayers block npc hits completely. Bones give xp when buried, or 2.5x when offered on the
boss altar before the boss is summoned. Prayer is always the second skill offered by xp lamps.

Potions all share one 3-cycle cooldown: prayer potions (restore 7 + 25% of base Prayer per dose),
Saradomin brews, super restores, blighted overloads and ambrosia. Blighted overloads hurt (2 damage
every 2 cycles, 5 times) but boost your combat stats for 500 cycles.

## Eating

Food has a 3-cycle eat delay, which also pushes back your next attack. Healing from any source is
capped at your base hitpoints level, except Saradomin brews and ambrosia.

## Magic

Every standard, Ancient and Arceuus combat spell works, and can be autocast with the right staff.
Burst and barrage hit up to 9 npcs around the target. Instant spells include Vengeance, Spellbook
Swap, Death Charge and the nine Resurrect thrall spells. An **arcane grimoire** from supply crates
switches between the standard, Ancient, Lunar and Arceuus spellbooks.
