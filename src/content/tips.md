# Your first run

You spawn at level 1 with 10 hitpoints, 25 purple sweets, a starter key and a looting bag. All xp
is multiplied by 100, so you level fast. The monsters get stronger just as fast.

## The first five minutes

1. **Open the nearest chest with your starter key.** Pick melee, ranged or magic. You get a
   bronze-tier kit and level 20 in that style's skills and Defence.
2. **Fight near your spawn.** The first monsters are level 2 or lower. Eat purple sweets (they heal
   1-3) when you're hurt.
3. **Pick up every key.** Bloody keys drop from 12% of kills and bloodier keys from 3%. Each opens
   a chest with three rewards to choose from.
4. **Search the supply crates.** Every map has 5. No key needed, and each gives 3 free rolls.
5. **Bury your bones.** You want protection prayers before the boss.

> **Your style sticks.** For the rest of the run, the style you pick is twice as likely as each
> other style in monster drops, and 6 times as likely in chest gear.

## Danger rises every 5 minutes

Danger level goes up by 1 every 5 minutes, and by 1 for every new world. New monsters spawn with
more hitpoints and bigger max hits (+55% for each level above 1), and more of them are alive at
once. The upside: chest lamps grow with danger too. See [Danger & the director](#/director).

## Before the boss

- **Get protection prayers** (level 37 for Protect from Magic, 40 for Missiles, 43 for Melee).
  Protection prayers block npc hits completely, and they're how you beat TzTok-Jad. Xp lamps
  always offer Prayer second.
- **Offer bones on the altar** for 2.5x the xp of burying them. It takes every bone of that type
  at once. The altar disappears when you summon the boss.
- **Stock up on food.** Sharks from late drops heal 20. Brews and ambrosia heal past your max.
- **Back off after summoning.** A new boss waits 5 ticks before it attacks.

## Food and potions

Eating takes 3 ticks and delays your next attack. Drinking a potion takes 3 ticks too (one
cooldown for all potions) but never delays an attack. Healing is capped at your max hitpoints,
except for brews and ambrosia. Boosted hitpoints drop by 1 every minute.

| Potion | Per dose |
|---|---|
| Prayer potion | Restores 7 + 25% of your Prayer level |
| Saradomin brew | Heals 15% + 2, boosts Defence 20% + 2, lowers Attack, Strength, Ranged and Magic 10% + 2 |
| Super restore | Restores 25% + 8 of every lowered stat, Prayer included |
| Blighted overload | Hits you for 2, five times over 10 ticks (needs 11 hitpoints to drink). For 5 minutes, boosts Attack and Strength 15% + 8, Ranged 10% + 7 and Magic 10% + 1, and lowers Defence 10% + 1 |
| Ambrosia | One dose. Fully heals and boosts hitpoints by 25% + 2, fully restores Prayer and boosts it by 20% + 5 |

The last dose leaves no empty vial.

## Magic

Every standard, Ancient and Arceuus combat spell works, and can be autocast with the right staff.
Burst and barrage spells hit up to 9 monsters around your target. Smoke spells poison, shadow
spells lower Attack, blood spells heal a quarter of the damage and ice spells freeze.

You can also cast Vengeance, Spellbook Swap, Death Charge and all nine Resurrect thrall spells.
The **arcane grimoire** (a 1 in 100 crate find) switches between all four spellbooks.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `::join name` | Join a friend's run. Only works in its first 5 minutes (danger level 1) |
| `::reset` | End the run for your whole team and start a new one |
| `::invincible` / `::vincible` | Take no damage / take damage again. For testing |
| `::world5` | Skip straight to the final world. For testing |
