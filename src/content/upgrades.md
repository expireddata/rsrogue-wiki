# Gear upgrades & life points

Enemies keep getting stronger for the whole run, so your gear can too. **Upgrade items** make a
piece of gear better for the rest of the run, and some of them raise your **life points** well past
99.

## Upgrade items

There are three kinds, each in seven tiers: bronze, iron, steel, mithril, adamant, rune and dragon.
They stack. Use one on a piece of equipment in your inventory to upgrade it.

| Item | Looks like | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Catalyst | A smithing catalyst | Multiplies every bonus of the gear |
| Offensive stone | Kebbit spikes | Adds accuracy, strength or magic damage (you pick) |
| Defensive stone | A magic stone | Adds defence or max life points (you pick) |

Each tier is coloured like its metal.

### Catalysts

Each catalyst adds to the gear's bonus multiplier, which starts at 1x:

| Tier | Bronze | Iron | Steel | Mithril | Adamant | Rune | Dragon |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Adds | +0.1x | +0.2x | +0.3x | +0.5x | +0.75x | +1x | +1.5x |

Only positive bonuses are multiplied: a platebody's
negative magic attack is never made worse.

### Stones

Using a stone asks what to add. An offensive stone asks for a style (melee, ranged or magic), then
accuracy or strength (magic damage for magic). A defensive stone offers melee, ranged or magic
defence, or life points. Melee accuracy and melee defence add to stab, slash and crush.

| Stat | Bronze | Iron | Steel | Mithril | Adamant | Rune | Dragon |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Accuracy | +3 | +5 | +8 | +12 | +18 | +26 | +36 |
| Melee or ranged strength | +2 | +3 | +5 | +7 | +10 | +14 | +20 |
| Magic damage | +0.5% | +1% | +1.5% | +2% | +3% | +4% | +6% |
| Defence | +4 | +6 | +10 | +15 | +22 | +32 | +45 |
| Life points | +4 | +6 | +10 | +15 | +22 | +32 | +45 |

Stones are added **before** catalysts multiply, so stones and catalysts on the same piece work
together.

### Spread them out

Each upgrade made to the same piece is worth **80%** of the one before it, down to 10%: the first
is worth its full value, the second 80%, the third 64%, and so on. Stone choices show what the
stone will actually add. So it pays to upgrade every piece of gear you wear rather than pile
everything onto your weapon: two steel catalysts on each of two pieces add +0.54x to each, while
four on one piece add +0.88x in all.

### Good to know

- Upgrades belong to the **kind** of gear: if you have two rune platebodies, they share upgrades.
- Upgraded gear **can't be dropped** or put in the looting bag. The trader still buys it, but its
  upgrades are lost once you have none of it left.
- Like everything else, upgrades end with the run.

## Where they come from

| Source | What you get |
|---|---|
| Npc drops | 6% per kill, bronze to adamant by the npc's loot level |
| Supply crates | In place of modifiers (crates no longer give modifiers), better as danger rises |
| Bosses | Every team member gets a catalyst, an offensive and a defensive stone (steel in world 1, one tier better each world), dropped under them |
| The wandering trader | Often sells a few, of its world's boss tier |

Key chests never give them.

## Life points

Your health is your **life points**: your Hitpoints level plus the life points on the gear you're
wearing. With a Hitpoints level of 105 and a platebody upgraded to +100 life points then made 3x as
good, you have **405** life points.

- Hits and hitsplats are in life points, so a 300 hit shows as 300.
- **Healing scales with your pool.** Food, potions and regeneration heal the same share of your
  life points as they would of your Hitpoints level. A shark heals much more at 405 than at 105.
- **Taking gear off keeps your share.** At 55 out of 405, taking off that platebody leaves you on 14
  out of 105 (rounded down, and never below 1).
- You're told your new max life points in chat whenever they change.
- The Hitpoints orb and skill show the **share** of your level, not your life points: at 55/405 with
  level 105 it reads 15/105.
