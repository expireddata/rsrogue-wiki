# Upgrades & life points

Monsters keep getting stronger, so your gear has to as well. Upgrade items make a piece of gear
better for the rest of the run, and defensive stones can push your health into the hundreds.

## Upgrade items

Three kinds, each in seven tiers coloured like their metal: bronze, iron, steel, mithril,
adamant, rune and dragon. They stack in your inventory. **Use one on a piece of equipment** to
upgrade it.

| Item | Looks like | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Catalyst | A smithing catalyst | Multiplies every bonus on the gear |
| Offensive stone | Kebbit spikes | Adds accuracy or damage (you pick) |
| Defensive stone | A magic stone | Adds defence or life points (you pick) |

### Catalysts

Each catalyst adds to the gear's bonus multiplier, which starts at 1x:

| Tier | Bronze | Iron | Steel | Mithril | Adamant | Rune | Dragon |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Adds | +0.1x | +0.2x | +0.3x | +0.5x | +0.75x | +1x | +1.5x |

Only positive bonuses are multiplied, so a platebody's magic penalty never gets worse.

### Stones

An offensive stone asks for a style (melee, ranged or magic), then accuracy or strength (magic
damage for magic). A defensive stone offers melee, ranged or magic defence, or life points. Melee
accuracy and defence cover stab, slash and crush at once.

| Stat | Bronze | Iron | Steel | Mithril | Adamant | Rune | Dragon |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Accuracy | +3 | +5 | +8 | +12 | +18 | +26 | +36 |
| Melee or ranged strength | +2 | +3 | +5 | +7 | +10 | +14 | +20 |
| Magic damage | +0.5% | +1% | +1.5% | +2% | +3% | +4% | +6% |
| Defence | +4 | +6 | +10 | +15 | +22 | +32 | +45 |
| Life points | +4 | +6 | +10 | +15 | +22 | +32 | +45 |

Stones are added **before** catalysts multiply, whatever order you use them in, so catalysts
multiply your stones too.

### Spread them out

Each upgrade on the same piece is worth **80%** of the one before it, down to a floor of 10%.
The stone menu shows what you'll actually get.

> **Example:** two steel catalysts on each of two pieces add +0.54x to both. Four on one piece add
> +0.88x to that piece alone. Upgrade everything you wear, not just your weapon.

### Good to know

- Upgrades belong to the **kind** of item. Two rune platebodies share the same upgrades.
- Upgraded gear **can't be dropped** or put in the looting bag. The trader still buys it, but the
  upgrades are gone once you hold none of that item.
- Upgrades end with the run, like everything else.

## Where they come from

| Source | What you get |
|---|---|
| Monsters | 6% of kills, bronze to adamant by the monster's loot level |
| Supply crates | Some rolls. Bronze at danger 1, one tier higher per danger level |
| Bosses | A catalyst, an offensive and a defensive stone for every member (steel in world 1, one tier better each world) |
| The wandering trader | Often sells a few, at the world's boss tier |

Key chests never give them.

## Life points

Your health is measured in **life points**: your Hitpoints level plus the life points on your
worn gear. Hitpoints level 105 and a platebody with +100 life points, then tripled by catalysts,
gives you **405** life points.

- Your hitpoints orb shows life points (405/405), and hits and hitsplats are in life points too.
- **Healing scales with your pool.** Food, potions and regeneration heal the same share of your
  life points as they would of your Hitpoints level, so a shark heals almost 4x as much at 405.
- **Taking gear off keeps your share.** At 55/405, removing that platebody leaves you at 14/105
  (rounded down, never below 1).
- Chat tells you your new max whenever it changes.
- The Hitpoints skill shows your share of your level: 55/405 at level 105 shows as 15/105.
