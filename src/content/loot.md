# Loot & supplies

Every monster rolls each drop separately, so one kill can drop gear, food, bones and a key all at
once.

| Drop | Chance | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Equipment | 45% | One piece of gear |
| Food | 50% | Usually 1-3 purple sweets |
| Bones | 50% | Better bones from stronger monsters |
| Bloody key | 12% | Opens a [chest](#/chests) |
| Bloodier key | 3% | Opens a chest, with better odds |
| Gear upgrade | 6% | A catalyst or stone, bronze to adamant ([Upgrades](#/upgrades)) |

Scavenger, Treasure hunter, Sweet tooth and Bounty [modifiers](#/modifiers) raise these chances
for the player credited with the kill (whoever dealt the most damage).

## Loot level

Drops scale with a monster's **loot level**: its combat level worked out from its current stats.
Monsters the director has scaled up have higher loot levels, so the later you are in a run, the
better everything drops.

## Equipment

- A random style: melee armour and weapons, ranged armour, bows, crossbows and ammo, or magic
  robes and staves. Your starter style is **twice as likely** as each of the other two.
- Tiers start one above the starter kits (iron, studded leather, Xerician) and climb with loot
  level. Sometimes you get a tier lower, and rarely a tier higher.
- Arrows and bolts drop 50 at a time. Ammo is never used up, so one stack lasts all run.

## Food

- **Purple sweets** most of the time: 1-3 of them, stackable, healing 1-3 each.
- **Solid food** a quarter of the time: shrimps, tuna, lobster, swordfish or shark, better with
  loot level.
- **A prayer potion** (1-4 doses) a fifth of the time.

## Bones

Bones, big bones, babydragon bones or dragon bones, by loot level. Bury them for Prayer xp, or
save them for the boss altar, which gives 2.5x.

## Boss drops

Every boss drops 3 pieces of equipment and 3 solid food, better in later worlds and at higher
danger. On top of that, **each member** gets:

- a **bloodier key**, a **catalyst**, an **offensive stone** and a **defensive stone**, dropped at
  their feet. Upgrades are steel in world 1 and one tier better each world after. A dead
  teammate's share drops where the boss died, for the team.
- a **35,000 xp lamp**, multiplied by their xp rate. At 100x that's 3.5 million xp.
