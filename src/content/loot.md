# Loot & food

Every npc that dies in an arena rolls its drops. Equipment, food, bones and keys are rolled
**independently**, so one kill can drop all of them.

| Drop | Chance | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Equipment | 45% | One piece of a random combat style |
| Food | 50% | Usually 1-3 purple sweets |
| Bones | 50% | Scale with the npc's loot level |
| Bloody key | 12% | Opens chests, favours xp and common modifiers |
| Bloodier key | 3% | Favours rare modifiers, gear and bigger lamps |

## Equipment

Tiers start one step above the starter kits (iron, studded leather, Xerician) and follow the npc's
**loot level**: its combat level worked out from its current stats, so scaled-up npcs drop better
gear. The drop is sometimes a tier lower and rarely a tier higher. Arrows and bolts drop as stacks
of 50. The style you picked from the starter chest is twice as likely as each other style.

## Food

Usually 1-3 purple sweets (stackable, heal 1-3). A quarter of the time it is one solid food that
scales with loot level: shrimps, tuna, lobster, swordfish or shark. A fifth of the time it is a
prayer potion of 1-4 doses.

## Bones

Bones, big bones, babydragon bones and dragon bones, by loot level.

## Boss drops

A boss drops 3 pieces of equipment and 3 solid food of a tier set by danger level and world, plus a
guaranteed **bloodier key**, and each member gets a 35k xp lamp.

## Loot modifiers

Scavenger, Treasure hunter, Sweet tooth and Bounty raise these chances for the player credited with
the kill. See [Modifiers](#/modifiers).
