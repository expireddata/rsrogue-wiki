# Skill capes

Reaching **base level 99** in a combat skill gives you that skill's cape, once per run. Like every
item, the capes are gone when the next run starts, and the trader never buys them.

| Cape | Perk while worn |
|---|---|
| Attack | +1 accuracy roll on every attack |
| Strength | +30% melee damage on npcs |
| Defence | Immortal's cooldown goes twice as fast |
| Ranged | Every ranged hit also hits attackable npcs within a tile of the target (up to 9), like a chinchompa |
| Prayer | Two protection prayers at once. A third turns off the oldest |
| Magic | "Spellbook" switches spellbook like the arcane grimoire, once every 5 minutes |
| Hitpoints | Every hit taken is halved (rounded down) |

Levels from any source count, and are checked regularly. Each cape is logged in the
[collection log](#/collection). Taking the **Maxed, but at what cost** [modifier](#/modifiers) sets
you to level 99 instantly, so it earns the capes too.
