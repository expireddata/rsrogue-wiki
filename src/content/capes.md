# Skill capes

Hit **base level 99** in a combat skill and you get its cape, once per run. Each
one has a perk that changes how you fight:

| Cape | Perk while worn |
|---|---|
| Attack | One extra accuracy roll on every attack |
| Strength | +30% melee damage against monsters |
| Defence | The [Immortal](#/modifiers) modifier recharges twice as fast |
| Ranged | Every ranged hit also hits each monster next to your target (up to 9), like a chinchompa. Each extra hit rolls its own accuracy and damage |
| Prayer | Two protection prayers at once. Turning on a third turns off the oldest |
| Magic | Its "Spellbook" option switches spellbook, like the arcane grimoire. Once every 5 minutes |
| Hitpoints | Every hit you take is halved (rounded down) |

> **Shortcut:** the **Maxed, but at what cost** [modifier](#/modifiers) sets your combat skills to
> 99 on the spot, so it hands you all seven capes. The catch: you never gain xp again that run.

Capes vanish at the end of the run, and the trader won't buy them. Each one is logged in your
[collection log](#/collection).
